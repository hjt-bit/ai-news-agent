The big picture: AI's foundations shook this week. Nvidia's founder-CEO resigned after reports of extramarital affairs. Microsoft 365 went down for 30 hours. Meanwhile, the new order moved: OpenAI gave its agents a wallet, Anthropic cut its top model's price 20%, and the Gulf kept building — HUMAIN's 3-trillion-parameter promise, up to $667M in new financing, a 10GW data-centre target.

The Lead

Huang resigns — Nvidia loses its founder-CEO

Why you care: Nvidia's founder-CEO of 33 years has resigned. The reported affair at the centre of it involves a woman US intelligence suspects of being a Chinese spy. CFO Colette Kress steps in as interim CEO. Q3 results still land Tuesday.

Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO and Board member. It took effect immediately. He co-founded Nvidia in 1993. His run lasted 33 years. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. She has been EVP and CFO since September 2013. She keeps the CFO role. Harvey C. Jones was appointed the board's first-ever Chair. The board is searching for a permanent CEO. It will consider internal and external candidates. Nvidia's own statement gives no reason. It says it expects operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap “to continue without interruption”. The reported reason is different. Huang admitted on Nov 8 to being unfaithful to his wife. He said he takes “full responsibility”. The reported affair at the centre of the story is with Alice Huang. Huang's Nov 8 admission of infidelity did not name her. She is a former executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that buys Nvidia chips. US intelligence officials suspect her of being a Chinese intelligence asset. She is not related to Jensen Huang. Megaspeed bought $4.8bn of Nvidia hardware in two years, The Times reported. US authorities say Alice Huang helped move Nvidia chips to Chinese firms. She has returned to China and is living in Beijing, Bloomberg reported. The US Department of Justice has not yet reached a conclusion on whether she was a spy. Attorney General Todd Blanche said the FBI is investigating. A second reported relationship came out on Nov 11. The New York Times reported an extramarital relationship with Kimberly Powell. She is Nvidia's vice-president of healthcare. The Times cited five people who worked at the company. Neither Huang nor Powell has confirmed it. Reuters reported a third: an intimate relationship with a second unnamed Nvidia employee. Reuters reports the affair disclosures were factors in his resignation. The board opened an independent investigation on Nov 8. It is looking into whether Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence operation. The board said it had no reason to believe US national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Q3 FY2027 results are still due Tuesday November 17, at about 1:20pm PT. Market reaction (reported, not Nvidia-stated): shares closed at $166.19. That is down 4.34% on November 12. They fell as much as ~14% during the day. AMD and Broadcom rose more than 5% the same day (reported).

Leader action: Ask your executive team which single person your AI strategy depends on most — and what the plan is if they leave tomorrow.

Strategic Briefing

Microsoft 365 outage downs Outlook for 30 hours

Why you care: Thirty hours without email: a Microsoft 365 outage that began Thursday knocked out Outlook, Teams calendars, Copilot's access to work data and SharePoint sharing. Microsoft says it was a technical issue, not cyber or AI.

The outage ran from Thursday November 12, 10:05 UTC to Friday November 13, 16:10 UTC. That is about 30 hours. The final update came Saturday November 14, at 04:17 UTC. The status was serviceRestored. What broke: Exchange Online mailbox access. That means Outlook on the web, desktop and mobile, plus delayed mail. Every feature that depends on Exchange was hit too. Teams calendars and scheduling. Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat. Copilot's access to work data. SharePoint external sharing. Defender XDR. Purview. Intune. Planner. To Do. Power BI. Graph. Core Teams calls, meetings and chats were mostly not affected. The cause (Microsoft, preliminary): an issue from an earlier network capacity upgrade. It surfaced after a recent network change. The systems that set up secure connections failed. Traffic shifts exposed a hidden bug. Connection requests piled up. The servers ran out of processing capacity. Microsoft says: “a technical issue not related to cyber or AI / agents.” A full post-incident report is promised. The preliminary report comes within 2 business days. The final report comes within 5. The scale (from Downdetector, not Microsoft): more than 130,000 Outlook problem reports at the US peak. They came from 74 countries.

Leader action: Ask your IT lead what your team's plan B is when email goes down for a day — and when it was last tested.

OpenAI gives its agents a wallet

Why you care: OpenAI just gave its agents a way to pay — without ever seeing your card. ChatGPT Wallet launched November 13. It is US-only, Pro subscribers only.

ChatGPT Wallet launched November 13. OpenAI calls it “the first wallet that lets agents pay without access to users' actual card details”. It needs a security check for every purchase. It keeps your card rewards and protections for eligible disputes. It was built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. It rolls out in dots, OpenAI's always-on agents. At first it is only for ChatGPT Pro subscribers. They must be 18 or older, in the US, with eligible US-issued cards. OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week. How it works: you add a card once. You review the items, total and card. You approve with Face ID or a fingerprint. The payment network then issues a token. The token is a substitute card number, security code and expiry. The agent and merchant never see your real card details. Full delegation is still in the future, with no date. OpenAI “expects” users will eventually pre-approve purchases. They would set spending limits, categories or time periods. A Commerce Partner Playbook was published alongside. Fraud-prevention partners are Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd. Payment providers are Adyen, Checkout.com and Stripe. OpenAI warns some sites may unintentionally block agent purchases. US-only at launch. There is no global rollout timetable.

Leader action: Ask your e-commerce lead whether your checkout will accept purchases made by an agent — and what your fraud rules do with them.

Anthropic launches Claude Fable and Mythos 5.2

Why you care: Anthropic's new top model is cheaper and faster — and it comes in two flavours. Fable 5.2 is open to all API users. Mythos 5.2 is the same model, restricted to vetted organisations. Anthropic's own benchmarks put it ahead of Opus 5.5 on coding — and nearly double on long-horizon work.

Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 launched November 9. They are the same underlying model. Fable is open to all API users. Mythos is restricted to vetted organisations. These are the cyber and life-sciences verification programmes. The price is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. That is down from $10/$50 for 5.1 — a 20% cut. Anthropic claims outputs are up to 30% faster. An optional fast mode costs $16/$80. It generates up to 2.5 times faster. Anthropic's own benchmarks (vendor-run, not independently verified): FrontierCode 2.0 scored 49.1% at Xhigh effort. Opus 5.5 scored 45.5%. Fable 5.1 scored 32.4%. The Remote Labor Index scored 41.3%. Opus 5.5 scored 21.3%.

Leader action: Ask your engineering lead to re-price your heaviest agent workloads on Fable 5.2 — and to verify Anthropic's benchmarks on your own tasks before switching.

HUMAIN promises 3-trillion-parameter model by December, full stack Dec 15

Why you care: HUMAIN's CEO says Saudi Arabia's full AI stack lands December 15. The headline is a 3-trillion-parameter model he calls the world's largest. His words — the proof isn't public yet.

The announcement came on Wednesday November 11 evening. It was at HUMAIN's Majdoul Tower offices in Riyadh. CEO Tareq Amin said the full AI stack will be available in Saudi Arabia on 15 December 2026. He said: “the world's largest open-weight model, 3 trillion parameters model, will be available in Saudi (Arabia), nobody in the planet has this.” He also said: “So we are bringing a state-of-the-art model that is 88 percent less cost and token consumptions than available models outside of the country.” And: “The complete stack, December 15, is not far away.” He said the stack took 15 months to build: “All of this is coming together, 15 months of work to build all of these layers is, to me, nothing but a miracle of what this team has done.” He added: “The software stack is built by Saudis, the cloud inferencing is built by Saudis, infrastructure deployed, operated by Saudis, nobody has done this.” At the same event, HUMAIN announced HUMAIN+. It is an applied AI education and training platform. It uses VUZ and immersive 360° video. Its operational launch is Q1 2027. The Riyadh campus has about 1,500 learner places a year. It has GPU-backed Builder Labs and four learning journeys. Confidence: these are Amin's own words, quoted by Arab News on Nov 12. The claims behind them have no published proof yet. No model name. No benchmark. No price. No published weights. December 15 is a date he stated, not a product that has shipped.

Leader action: Ask your Saudi lead what a Saudi-built 3-trillion-parameter model would change for your data-residency plans — and to wait for December 15 before betting on it.

MBZUAI's IFM releases open world model PAN 2

Why you care: Abu Dhabi's Institute of Foundation Models released PAN 2. It is an open world model. It simulates a video of what happens next, from a scene and a text action. The weights and code are open, under Apache 2.0. On its own reported results, it ranks third overall on Physics-IQ, first among open models.

The Institute of Foundation Models (IFM) released PAN 2 on 10 November 2026. IFM was established by MBZUAI. PAN 2 is an open, general-purpose world model. Give it a starting scene and a text action. It simulates a video of what happens next. It is “grounded in physics, time, and space”. This is simulative reasoning, for agents and robotics. It was released with video model weights, full training and inference code, a technical blog and documentation. All of it is under Apache 2.0. IFM's reported results (self-reported, no independent verification): 3rd overall on Physics-IQ Verified. 1st among open-weight models. It placed just behind MiniMax-H3, ahead of four other models, across six dimensions. It completed more full plans than any other model tested in multi-step planning. IFM itself notes that long-term world consistency “remains an open challenge.” IFM launched in May 2025 by MBZUAI. It has labs in Abu Dhabi, Silicon Valley and Paris.

Leader action: Ask your robotics or agent lead whether a world model that simulates outcomes before acting changes how your agents plan — and to test PAN 2 against your own tasks.

Consumer Signals

OpenAI ships GPT-5.1, warmer and faster

Why you care: GPT-5.1 is here — warmer and faster. Instant thinks before responding, and Thinking is roughly twice as fast on the fastest tasks. In most cases, OpenAI says, you won't need to choose a model at all.

GPT-5.1 launched November 12. That is the reported date — the page itself carries no date. GPT-5.1 Instant is the most-used model. It is warmer, more intelligent and better at following instructions. It uses adaptive reasoning to “think before responding”. GPT-5.1 Thinking is the advanced reasoning model. It is easier to understand. It is faster on simple tasks and more persistent on complex ones. It is “roughly twice as fast on the fastest tasks and twice as slow on the slowest tasks”. GPT-5.1 Auto keeps routing each query. “In most cases, you won't need to choose a model at all.” The rollout: paid plans first (Pro, Plus, Go, Business). Then free and logged-out users, gradually, over the next few days. Enterprise and Edu get a 7-day early-access toggle. It is off by default. After that window, GPT-5.1 “will become the sole default model”. GPT-5 (Instant and Thinking) stays in the legacy-models dropdown for paid subscribers. It stays for 3 months. New tone controls: Default, Friendly (formerly Listener) and Efficient (formerly Robot) remain. Professional, Candid and Quirky are added. Cynical and Nerdy are unchanged. Granular tuning of characteristics is an experiment. It is “starting to roll out gradually later this week… starting with a limited number of users”. Personalisation changes now “take effect across all chats right away, including ongoing conversations”. API: GPT-5.1 Instant is live as gpt-5.1-chat-latest and Thinking as gpt-5.1. Both are available to developers on all paid tiers. GPT-5 Pro becomes GPT-5.1 Pro “soon”. OpenAI claims “significant improvements on math and coding evaluations like AIME 2025 and Codeforces”. It gives no numbers in the page.

Leader action: Ask your team to try the new tone controls — Professional, Candid, Quirky — and pick one house voice before everyone picks their own.

Anthropic puts whiteboards and spreadsheets inside Claude

Why you care: Claude just grew a whiteboard, a spreadsheet and a data layer. Whiteboards and Sheets launched in beta. Dashboards is now generally available as Claude Data.

Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets launched in beta on November 10. Claude Dashboards becomes generally available as Claude Data. All three are “available on all paid plans,” on web, desktop and mobile. Whiteboards: a shared board where a team brainstorms with Claude. Claude “can turn it into a board with a timeline with owners and dates”. It groups retro notes by theme and drafts action items. A board can become a doc or deck. Or it can move to Lucid, Miro or Vani by Zoho. Sheets: Claude “generate[s] spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting, and drop-downs, often from just a prompt”. It edits in cells, outlining each edit. Sheets are shareable. “For now, sheets have simple spreadsheet functionality”. Export to .xlsx or CSV. Or continue in Airtable, Asana, monday.com or Smartsheet. Data was renamed from Dashboards. Dashboards was in beta since October. It connects to Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, ClickHouse and others. Data adds data stories and narrated videos, certified-metric badges, design systems, per-dashboard caching and refresh, and tracked changes with rollback. Certified queries and live sharing remain in beta. Partners: Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog, Sigma and ThoughtSpot. The same day, interactive connectors “can open as full apps beside your conversation or in full screen”. These include Adobe, Atlassian, Box and Notion. Admin defaults: on Team plans, all three are on by default. On Enterprise plans, Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default. Claude Data “turns on by default on November 20, 2026”.

Leader action: Ask your team to run the next retro on a Claude Whiteboard — Claude groups the notes by theme and drafts the action items.

Google brings Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10

Why you care: The November Pixel Drop puts Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10. Rambler cleans up your voice typing. Gemini takes on multi-step tasks. Call Screen gets more conversational. One catch: the multi-step tasks aren't in the UAE yet.

The November Pixel Drop began November 10. “Updates start rolling out today and will continue over the next several weeks.” Gemini Intelligence is now available on Pixel 10 devices. It brings Rambler, personalised suggestions and multi-step tasks. Rambler is a voice typing upgrade. Call Screen upgrade on the Pixel 11 series: Gemini asks unknown callers why they are reaching out “in a much more conversational manner”. You get a live transcript. Pixel Tag: “available November 11”. It costs $29.99 for one, or $99.99 for a four-pack. It is slim enough for a wallet. Its “battery that lasts over a year”. The Find Hub network has “more than 1 billion Android devices”. UWB gives visual direction and distance nearby.
